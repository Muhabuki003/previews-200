A dining table sized for your exact room. A desk built for a specific corner. A bed that's exactly the height you want. That's what custom furniture in Houston is for.

Furniture Built Around Your Space

Store-bought furniture comes in fixed sizes and a handful of finishes. Custom furniture flips that around: you tell us the dimensions, the wood species and the style, and we build a piece that fits the room — and your life — perfectly.

What We Build

  • Dining tables — solid hardwood tops with a finish that handles daily use.
  • Desks — sized for your workspace, with the storage and cable access you need.
  • Shelving — floating shelves, bookcases and display units built to the inch.
  • Beds and headboards — platform beds and statement headboards in your species of choice.
  • Live-edge pieces — natural-edge tables and slabs that become the room's focal point.

Choosing the Right Wood

The species you choose shapes both the look and the price:

  • Walnut — rich, dark and warm; a premium look.
  • White oak — hard, stable and versatile; great for dining tables.
  • Maple — light, clean and durable; ideal for desks and shelving.
  • Cherry — deepens in color over time; a classic, warm finish.

Not sure? We'll walk you through the options based on how the piece will be used and where it will live.

What Affects the Cost

Three things drive the price of custom furniture: size (more material and labor), species (walnut and exotic woods cost more than maple), and complexity (live-edge tops, drawers and joinery add time). You'll always get a firm quote before we start.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom furniture cost in Houston?

Custom furniture cost depends on size, wood species and complexity. A solid hardwood dining table costs more than a softwood shelf, and live-edge or exotic species add cost. A good shop gives a firm quote before starting, so there are no surprises.

What wood should I choose for a dining table?

White oak and walnut are popular for dining tables — both are hard, stable and look great with a natural or oiled finish. Maple and cherry are excellent too. The right choice balances look, durability and budget.

How long does custom furniture take to build?

A simple table or desk often takes two to four weeks from order to delivery, while a larger or more detailed piece can take longer. Your timeline is confirmed up front when the piece is specced.
