Custom Cabinets in Houston: Built to Fit, Built to Last

Stock cabinets leave gaps. Custom cabinets use every inch — built to your exact walls, in the wood and finish you choose, and built to hold up in Houston's humidity.

Why Custom Cabinets

Stock cabinets come in a few fixed widths, so installers fill the leftover space with spacers and filler strips — wasted inches you paid for. Custom cabinets are measured to your walls and built to fit them exactly. No gaps, no dead space, no compromise.

What We Build

  • Kitchen cabinets — base, wall and tall cabinets laid out around your appliances and workflow.
  • Bathroom vanities — sized for your bathroom, with storage that actually works.
  • Built-in storage — mudroom benches, laundry cabinets, garage and pantry systems.
  • Bookcases and media units — wall-to-wall built-ins that turn dead space into storage.

Materials That Matter

The material is the difference between cabinets that last decades and ones that sag in five years:

  • Hardwood plywood — the standard for cabinet boxes; stable and warp-resistant in humid climates.
  • Solid hardwood — for doors, drawer fronts and face frames; walnut, oak, maple and cherry.
  • MDF — a budget option for painted doors, but less durable than hardwood.

We'll recommend the right combination for your budget and how the space will be used.

Custom vs. Semi-Custom vs. Stock

  • Stock — fixed sizes, fixed finishes; gaps filled with spacers.
  • Semi-custom — fixed boxes with some size and finish options; still limited.
  • Custom — built to your exact dimensions, species and finish; no wasted space.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between custom and stock cabinets?

Stock cabinets come in fixed sizes and must be filled in with spacers, leaving gaps and dead space. Custom cabinets are built to your exact wall dimensions, so every inch is used and the fit is seamless.

What material is best for custom cabinets?

Hardwood plywood is the workhorse for cabinet boxes because it resists warping in humid climates like Houston. Solid hardwood is used for doors, drawer fronts and face frames. MDF can work for painted doors but is less durable than hardwood.

How long does custom cabinetry take?

A single vanity or small built-in can take a few weeks, while a full kitchen typically runs longer because of the number of boxes, doors and the finishing schedule. Your timeline is set up front.
