⊕FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a new website?

No. If the current site works, we wire the layer into it. If it doesn't load fast or ask the right questions, we rebuild it as part of the same build.

What does “under 60 seconds” actually mean?

It's the reply target the layer is built to: an enquiry that lands while you're closed gets an answer with prices, hours, next steps and a booking link, before the customer tries the next business on the list.

Who writes the replies?

We do, with you, at setup — one reply per service in your voice, with your prices and your hours. You can edit any of them from the inbox at any time.

What happens to my data if we stop?

The domain, the content and the customer list are yours. Ask once and it's exported, and the site keeps running on hosting you control.
