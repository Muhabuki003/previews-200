How it works
The ribbon wraps through damp hair and holds it in a soft twist overnight. Because nothing is heated, the wave is set by the wrap rather than by a hot barrel — looser if you wrap wide, tighter if you wrap small.

Why it works

The wave is set while you sleep, not while you stand at the mirror

No heat at all

There is no barrel, no plate and no temperature to pick. Nothing in the set gets hot, so there is nothing to singe or press a hard kink into.

Soft enough to lie on

Foam core under a satin cover, with scrunchies at the ends instead of metal. It is designed to be slept in, which is the only way an overnight set ever works.

Loose or defined, your call

Wrap wide sections for a soft beach wave, or take smaller twists for a tighter curl. Same ribbon, same night.

How to use it

Four steps, then go to sleep

These follow the instruction card in the box: slightly damp hair, clip at the crown, wrap both sides, unwind in the morning.

01

Start slightly damp

Towel-dried, not dripping. Bone-dry hair works too — the set is simply firmer on hair with a little moisture in it.

02

Clip it at the crown

Fix the ribbon at the middle of the head so both tails fall either side, and clip the short pieces out of the way.

03

Wrap and tie off

Twist each side around its tail of the ribbon and secure the ends with the scrunchies. The headband keeps the top section clear.

04

Sleep, then unwind

Leave it in overnight. In the morning, unwind from the ends and comb through with your fingers — no heat to re-set it.

Questions

The things people ask before buying

Will it work on my hair?
It is sold as suitable for most hair lengths and textures. The ribbon is one size, so long hair takes a looser, softer wave and shorter hair takes a tighter one.
Does it damage your hair?
No heat is used anywhere in the process, so there is nothing to singe and no hot plate to press a kink into. That is the entire reason the set exists.
Can you really sleep in it?
That is what it is made for. The core is foam, the outside is satin, and the ends tie off with scrunchies rather than clipping metal against your head.
Damp or dry hair?
The manufacturer's instructions start with slightly damp hair, towel-dried rather than wet. Dry hair works as well — the set is just firmer when there is a little moisture in it.
