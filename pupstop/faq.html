Sizing

Getting the fit right first time

Measure the ribcage, not the neck. If your dog sits between two sizes, go up — the straps adjust down but not past their limit.

Small fits 5–10 kg, Medium 10–25 kg, Large 25–40 kg. Measure around the widest part of the ribcage, just behind the front legs, and match that number to the size band.

Snug enough that you can slide two fingers under a strap, and no more. Loose kit is the usual reason a dog slips out of a harness, not the harness itself.

Go up. You can always tighten a size up, but you cannot lengthen a size down. If you are still unsure, send us the measurement and we will pick for you.

Safety

What it does, and its limit

The honest version, because this is the part that matters.

It has no independent crash-test certificate, and we will not imply one. What it does is hold your dog in their seat and spread load across the chest instead of the throat. That is a large improvement over an unrestrained dog, which is the comparison that matters most.

Back seat, facing forward, buckled in like a passenger. Never in the front seat where an airbag deploys, and never loose in the boot.

Yes. The tether is short enough to keep them off the front seats and long enough to stand, turn around and settle.
