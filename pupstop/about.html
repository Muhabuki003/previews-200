About

We started with the seat belt, not the dog bed.

PUPSTOP makes a short list of dog car products. The first one is a harness that works with the belt your car already has.

Most dog travel gear starts somewhere else. A crate starts with the boot. A clip-on strap starts with the collar. Both then try to meet the car halfway, and the seat belt ends up as an afterthought.

We started at the belt. A standard lap-and-shoulder belt is a known, tested geometry — it is the one part of the car that was designed to hold a body in a crash. A harness that routes that belt across a padded chest plate gets to borrow some of that engineering instead of inventing its own.

That is the whole idea. It is not complicated, and we would rather keep it that way than add features that make the product worse at its one job.

What we will and will not claim

We will tell you the harness holds your dog in their seat and spreads load across the chest rather than the throat. We will not tell you it has a crash-test rating, because it does not have a certificate to point at. An unrestrained dog is the real comparison, and the harness is a great deal better than that.

We will show you an empty reviews section until real customers write reviews. We will not run a countdown timer, and we will not tell you stock is almost gone to move a decision along.

How the range grows

One product at a time. Each one gets its own page in the same layout, so you always know where the price, the sizes, the shipping window and the fit notes live. If a product does not earn its place, it gets cut rather than kept alive on discount.

Where the photos come from

The photography on this site is free-to-use stock standing in for our own product shots. It shows dogs in cars, not our harness specifically — the config flags every one of these as a preview image. Real photography replaces them before launch.

PUPSTOP is a new, small operation. Questions go to the contact page.
