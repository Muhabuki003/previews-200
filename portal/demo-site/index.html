About Me

Retired Houston on-air personality, licensed auctioneer, and your event’s biggest fan.

Johnny ‘Bravo’ Holloway a 2nd generation native Houstonian, is a retired 16 year Houston on-air personality with 104.1 KRBE morning show, ESPN radio 97.5 and 89.3 KSBJ radio.

Growing up in Memorial, he was introduced at a very early age to the value of participating in non-profit organizations. His grandfather, Jack Holloway, was one of the founders for the non-profit Spring Branch Memorial Sports Association. He has a passion for helping people evidenced by an impressive statistic of assisting in raising nearly 10 million dollars annually for over 50 different non-profits in our community.
